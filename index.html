ABOUT US

Making Library Attendance
Simple and Organized.

The Library Attendance Monitoring System was created to provide teachers with a faster and more organized way of monitoring student library attendance.

Instead of relying on slow manual sign-in records, the system provides one central platform where teachers can manage students, record attendance, and view reports.
